# Author-written research logs

These files are byte-preserving snapshots of the researcher's host-side logs.
They record hypotheses, decisions, interpretation, and the evolution of the
project. They have not been retrospectively copy-edited to make the research
trajectory look cleaner.

| Snapshot | Role | Current interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [`EXP-001.md`](EXP-001.md) | original intermediate-layer experiment | Protocol/result interpretation superseded by EXP-002/003. |
| [`EXP-002.md`](EXP-002.md) | probe-failure diagnosis | Complete diagnostic evidence. |
| [`EXP-003.md`](EXP-003.md) | valid-probe verification | Confirmatory within its model/task scope. |
| [`EXP-004.md`](EXP-004.md) | fixed-readout/path search | H1 discovery evidence plus confirmatory H2. |
| [`POST-EXP-003-DIRECTION-HISTORY.md`](POST-EXP-003-DIRECTION-HISTORY.md) | research direction history | Context, not hypothesis evidence. |

Use [`../docs/ERRATA.md`](../docs/ERRATA.md) for known corrections and
[`../docs/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md`](../docs/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) for current claim
strength. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records original filenames, byte sizes, and
SHA-256 hashes so that future syncs cannot silently alter a snapshot.
