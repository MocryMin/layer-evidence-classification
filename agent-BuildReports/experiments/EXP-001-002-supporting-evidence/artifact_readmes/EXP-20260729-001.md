# EXP-20260729-001 - Artifacts

Frozen-backbone pilot for intermediate-layer recoverability on DeBERTa-v3-base /
CLINC150. The mainline (12 layers x 10 seeds) was **paused** after the lr smoke
test revealed mid-layer collapse; the pivot diagnostics live in
EXP-20260729-002 (see `artifacts/EXP-20260729-002/README.md` and the report at
`agent-BuildReports/experiments/EXP-20260729-002--linear-probe-midlayer-collapse.md`).

## Provenance

- Backbone: `microsoft/deberta-v3-base` (frozen, 12 layers, hidden 768), CLS pooling.
- Dataset: CLINC150 `plus`, OOS (label 42) dropped, 150 in-scope classes, splits
  15000/3000/4500. Prompt `Classify the intent: {utterance}`.
- Resolved config: `run_config.yaml` (lr=1e-3, selected by the validation-only
  smoke test; AdamW, wd=0.01, batch=256, 100 epochs, grad_clip=1.0, plain
  `linear_with_bias` head, frozen backbone, seed list in `seeds.json`).

## Key results for the report

### Collapse discovery - `smoke_lr_results.json`

Validation-only smoke test, layers {1,6,12}, seed 17, lr candidates
{1e-5, 1e-4, 1e-3}. Best val acc per (lr, layer):

| lr | L1 | L6 | L12 | mean |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1e-3 | 0.067 | 0.028 | 0.607 | 0.234 (selected) |
| 1e-4 | 0.031 | 0.023 | 0.329 | 0.127 |
| 1e-5 | 0.009 | 0.007 | 0.076 | 0.031 |

Mid layers stay near-random for every lr; **lr=1e-3 selected** (max mean val acc).
This is the run that triggered the pause and the EXP-002 diagnostics.

### Full 12-layer collapse curve - `plain_probe_mainline/results.json`

Post-hoc run of the paused mainline (lr=1e-3, 10 seeds, 100 epochs), to record
the per-layer collapse curve the original pause skipped. Reproduces
`smoke_lr_results.json` exactly on seed-17 layers {1,6,12} (consistency check
passes to 1e-4). Per-layer mean val/test acc over 10 seeds:

| layer | val acc | test acc | layer | val acc | test acc |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.071 ± 0.005 | 0.068 | 7 | 0.076 ± 0.012 | 0.074 |
| 2 | 0.129 ± 0.002 | 0.144 | 8 | 0.035 ± 0.006 | 0.034 |
| 3 | 0.243 ± 0.005 | 0.252 | 9 | 0.494 ± 0.003 | 0.487 |
| 4 | 0.064 ± 0.002 | 0.064 | 10 | 0.158 ± 0.014 | 0.162 |
| 5 | 0.091 ± 0.004 | 0.092 | 11 | 0.574 ± 0.005 | 0.558 |
| 6 | 0.026 ± 0.002 | 0.025 | 12 | 0.608 ± 0.003 | 0.585 |

Mid layers (4-8) collapse to near-random (L6 worst, 0.026); the small std shows
the collapse is seed-robust (structural), not a bad seed. Test tracks val.

## File schemas

- `smoke_lr_results.json`: `candidates`, `layers`, `seed`, `selected_lr`,
  `results[]` = `{lr, mean_val_acc, mean_val_nll, per_layer{str(L):{best_val_acc,
  best_val_nll}}}`.
- `plain_probe_mainline/results.json`: `config`, `per_layer_seed{str(L):
  {str(seed): {best_epoch, best_val_acc, best_val_nll, test_acc, test_nll,
  val_history[]}}}`, `per_layer_summary{str(L): {val/test_acc_{mean,std,min,max}}}`.
- `cache/*.safetensors`: `hidden` (N, 12, 768) float16, `labels` (N,) int64,
  `sample_ids` (N,) int64.

## Reproduction

Re-run the mainline probe via `python -u scripts/exp001_plain_probe.py` (12
layers x 10 seeds, lr=1e-3, deterministic given the seed list). The lr smoke
test is `python -u scripts/run_smoke_lr.py` (uses `src/pipeline.run_smoke_lr`).
The collapse is cross-referenced with feature stats and rescue-method accuracies
in `artifacts/EXP-20260729-002/02_variance_collapse/per_layer_collapse_summary.json`.
